About Us

Having academic troubles? Better call Surfers! Surfers provides focused academic support for biology students. We offer content tutoring for first- to third-year biology modules, applied statistical analyses using SPSS, and detailed feedback on academic writing.

Our Services

Biology Tutoring (1st–3rd Year)

  • One-on-one and small-group tutoring
  • Concept clarification for core biology modules
  • Test and examination preparation
  • Integration of theory and practical work

Statistical Analyses (SPSS)

  • Introduction to SPSS for beginners
  • Choosing appropriate statistical tests
  • Data entry, cleaning, and analysis
  • Interpretation of outputs for reports

Academic Writing & Review

  • Project write-up reviews for both undergraduates and postgraduates
  • Practical report editing and feedback
  • Assignment structure and clarity
  • Alignment with university standards
